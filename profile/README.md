# Pakistan Air Quality Initiative (PAQI)

Open air quality data, monitoring and research for Pakistan since 2016. We run a community
monitoring network, publish every reading openly, and build the tools below.

| Project | What it is | Live |
|---|---|---|
| [paqi_hawanama](https://github.com/PakAirQuality/paqi_hawanama) | **Hawanama**: real-time air quality map and open PM2.5 / AQI data for Pakistan and South Asia (1,200+ monitors, 600+ cities, hourly) | [hawanama.org](https://hawanama.org) · [data and API](https://hawanama.org/about/) |
| [pakistan-emissions-sectors](https://github.com/PakAirQuality/pakistan-emissions-sectors) | **PakEmis**: a ten-sector greenhouse gas and air pollutant emissions inventory for Pakistan, FY2015-16 to FY2023-24 | [emissions.hawanama.org](https://emissions.hawanama.org) |
| [pak_quality_estimation](https://github.com/PakAirQuality/pak_quality_estimation) | Satellite-supported daily PM2.5 mapping for Pakistan under sparse monitoring: code, model artifacts and reproducibility package | [doi:10.5281/zenodo.20824536](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20824536) |

Also from PAQI: [Unveiling Pakistan's Air Pollution](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17629179), a national
landscape report on health risks, sources and solutions; the PAQI monitoring archive on
[NASA Earthdata](https://asdc.larc.nasa.gov/project/Low-Cost%20Sensor%20AQ/Low-Cost-Sensors-AQ_PakAirQuality_1);
and the [Unveiling Karachi's Air](https://github.com/PakAirQuality/Unveiling-Karachi-Air) dataset.
